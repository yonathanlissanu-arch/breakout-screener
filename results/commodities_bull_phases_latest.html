Bull Market Phase Guide

Early Bull — 200-day MA recently turned up after a prolonged decline or flat base. Price just crossed above 200-day MA (typically <1 year ago). Not yet extended. Best risk/reward entry zone.
Early-Mid Bull — Transitional: bull trend confirmed but 200-day MA slope still modest. Days above 200-day MA 6–18 months. Good entry.
Mid Bull — 200-day MA rising steadily for 1–3 years. Price 15–50% above 200-day MA. RSI healthy 55–70. Trend is established; pullbacks are buying opportunities.
Late Bull — Price >50% above 200-day MA or RSI >80. Parabolic or near-parabolic. Higher risk of mean-reversion. Reduce or wait for pullback.
Recovery — Price near or below 200-day MA; trend not yet confirmed. Watch for 200-day MA to flatten and turn up.
Bear — 200-day MA declining, price below key support. Avoid.
